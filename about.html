About the Startup

AI-powered sleep coach named SleepWell. Monitors sleep patterns via wearable devices and smartphone sensors. Provides personalized sleep schedules, relaxation routines, and AI-guided meditation for better rest. Includes sleep analytics and habit tracking to improve long-term sleep quality. Target audience: adults aged 20-45, tech-savvy, struggling with sleep and looking for healthier routines.
